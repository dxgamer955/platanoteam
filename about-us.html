01 Our story

Since 2015

A small idea became
a growing universe.

Platano Team is an independent development studio specializing in multi-genre, 2D pixel-art games.

Founded by Eric Roquel Correa with the goal of creating video games made by Puerto Ricans, the studio began releasing games across Android and the web in 2017. Every project is a chance to experiment, learn, and tell a new story.
