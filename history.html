The timeline

Ten years,
one step at a time.

Not every year brought a finished release. Some brought prototypes, technical lessons, a new direction, or simply the determination to continue.

2015

The beginning

A Puerto Rican dream becomes Platano Team.

Growing up, Eric Roquel Correa rarely saw Puerto Rican game studios or locally made games recognized around the world. He founded Platano Team to help change that, to create original games from his hometown, become part of Puerto Rico’s game-development history, and show the world what creators from the island can build.

2017—2019

Going public

Experiments become releases.

Platano Team moved beyond private prototypes and began publishing games for the web and Android, learning from every build and every player.

2020—2021

A wider toolkit

The work expands beyond one kind of project.

New tools, platforms, visual identities, and technical experiments broadened what Platano Team could build while games remained at its center.

2022

Old hardware, new ideas

Nintendo DS homebrew joins the story.

Jovani Vazquez: The Game DS brought Platano Team to original Nintendo DS hardware and opened a new chapter of homebrew development.

2024

A new identity

The current logo arrives.

2025

Ten years

A decade of making, learning, and starting again.

The tenth anniversary was not an ending. It marked ten years of independent creation and a foundation for larger games, software, hardware, music, and experimental work.
