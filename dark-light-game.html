Dark Light is a pixel-art platforming adventure inspired by NES classics from the 1980s and 1990s.

John's quiet night is shattered by a mysterious lightning strike and by monsters emerging from the darkness. Explore the island, unlock new abilities, and uncover the forgotten lore behind the anomaly threatening his world.

Explore

Travel across an island filled with dangerous places, hidden secrets, and new abilities.

Battle

Face monstrous enemies and bosses using the improvised tools John finds.

Uncover

Solve puzzles and piece together the source of the dark anomaly.
