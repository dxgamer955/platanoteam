The game

Between shadow and light

Dark Light is a pixel-art platforming adventure inspired by NES classics from the 1980s and 1990s. John’s quiet night is shattered by a mysterious lightning strike—and by monsters emerging from the darkness.

Explore the island, fight enemies and bosses, solve puzzles, unlock new abilities, and uncover the forgotten lore behind the anomaly threatening John’s world.
